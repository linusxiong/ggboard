## Open issues

- HeroUI 3.2.6 hard-codes English accessible names: Tabs scroll buttons ("Scroll tabs left/right/up/down"), Spinner
  ("Loading"), Autocomplete ("Clear selection"), Tag ("Remove tag"). Close buttons are wrapped; the others need an
  upstream prop or a patch.
- Payment gateway field labels/descriptions come from the Go plugins' metadata in mixed Chinese/English (EPay
  "Merchant id", "MD5 key"); needs localized metadata or frontend overrides.
- The prerendered markup reflects a fresh install (zh-CN, signed out, email verification and passkeys off). Sites with
  a different default locale, email verification or passkeys briefly paint the default markup until the app replaces
  it (possible small layout shift on `/register`).
- Signed-in pages are not prerendered; `/subscription` mobile performance is 99 (TBT ≈ 110 ms from the rule builder).
- Admin node list truncates `host:port` at 1440 px (cosmetic).
